### 3. Cut further

- **Drop PR 5 (Guard → Oxlint conversion):** The 13 candidate guard files take **1.28 s** total and already run inside the Vitest shards. Porting them to AST rules in `lint/agentsRules.mjs` adds complex plugin code and slows down both `yarn lint` and `lint/agentsRules.test.ts`.
- **Fold `schema-dump-check` and `schema-dump-check-pg` as steps of `test-pg`, not Vitest tests:** `test-pg` already runs the PG 17 service container. Running the SQLite dump diff (~3 s), PG 17 dump + Kysely type diff (~10 s), and PG 14 baseline check as steps in `test-pg` deletes both jobs (~1.5 runner-min) without coupling local `yarn test` to host `sqlite3`/`pg_dump` binaries.
- **Go straight to Phase B for `test-pg` (`05-plan.md:15`):** Remove the 5 SQLite-only files and the ~150 route/action/job/page tests in `test-pg` that only call `Database` facade methods already tested on PG in `lib/database/**`. Running only `lib/database/**`, `lib/services/auth/**`, and `scripts/backup/**` (~215 files) cuts `test-pg` from **10.18 min to ~5.5 min**, immediately saving the full **2.17 min** of CI wall time (`01-ci-inventory.md:79`).
- **Trim setup in the top 10 slowest files (`02-metrics.md:46-60`):** 10 files (`post-box.drafts.test.tsx` at 59.5 s, `status-reply-box.drafts.test.tsx` at 23.6 s, `exportAccountArchiveJob.test.ts` at 21.4 s, etc.) take **183 s (3.05 min)**—more than all 775 planned deletions combined.

### 5. Verdict

`05-plan.md` sets the right target architecture—four SQLite shards plus one generated PostgreSQL job with schema-dump checks folded in—and its strict assertion-containment rule is the right standard, but several mechanical assumptions do not match the repo. Keying PG selection on `createTestDatabase` misses 107 current `test-pg` files using `getTestDatabaseWithInstance`/`getTestDatabaseTable`; PR 3 misclassifies 50 general `app/api` duplicates as `routeScopeWiring` 401/scope cases and targets a protected canary (`preferences/route.test.ts`); 2 of the 5 whole-file RCU deletions (`domainScope.test.ts`, `statusFitnessFiles.test.ts`) drop untested edge cases; 6 RCU trims shift coverage from PG-capable unit tests to SQLite-only route tests; and PR 5's guard-to-oxlint conversion costs more time in `lint/agentsRules.test.ts` than the 1.28 s it removes. Fix the 2 failing zip tests first, land PR 6's generated PG selection and step-folded schema dumps second, drop PR 5, and merge PRs 3 and 4 with explicit checks against A↔B duplicate cycles and PG coverage loss.
